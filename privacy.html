Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

LifeSquares does not collect any personal data. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data you create

Goals, milestones, days off and colors you enter are stored only on your device. Widgets read the same data from a shared container on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

LifeSquares has no sign-up and no account. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or any third-party SDKs, and it does not access your contacts, location, photos or other personal information.

Backups

Your app data may be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which are managed by Apple according to your own settings. The developer has no access to them.

Deleting your data

Delete a goal in the app to remove it, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you and are not shared with anyone.

Children

LifeSquares does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
