Common questions

Why is there no forecast yet?
The forecast appears after you close your first milestone and about a fifth of the goal's working days have passed. Until then there isn't enough data for an honest estimate.

Why can't I make a goal shorter?
A goal can't end before its last milestone. Move or delete that milestone first, then shorten the goal.

Is my data backed up?
Your goals are stored only on your device and are included in your regular iPhone backup. LifeSquares has no account and no server.
